Web app

nostrtx

Bitcoin transactions for Nostr keys. Paste an npub, get its taproot address, build an unsigned PSBT, sign it on your hardware — never in the browser — and broadcast. Works on both chains since the split — XBT (BLAKE2b) and BTC — and splits your coins between them. Multisig included. Entirely client-side: no backend, no custody, no account.

Open nostrtx Source

Features

npub → BitcoinEvery Nostr key is a taproot address. Send sats to anyone you follow.
Unsigned PSBTs onlyThe app never touches a private key. Sign on the Pocket Signer via USB, bridge, or SD card.
MultisigBuild shared taproot wallets with co-signers, collect signatures, finalize, broadcast.
Fee controlSlow / normal / fast presets from the mempool, or enter a custom sat/vB rate — with a live cost preview before you build.
Coin controlPick exactly which UTXOs to spend.
XBT and BTCOne switch. Your address is loaded on both chains and every coin is tagged unsplit, XBT only, or BTC only.
Split coinsOne tap builds the XBT transaction that separates your coins: signed SIGHASH_UNIFIED, valid on XBT only, never replayable to BTC. After it confirms, spend the BTC side freely.
Replay-safe by defaultEvery XBT transaction opts in to SIGHASH_UNIFIED; a BTC spend of unsplit coins is refused until you acknowledge it also moves the XBT.
Follows built inYour Nostr follow list is the address book.

How to use

  1. Open nostrtx.com and log in (NIP-07 extension or bunker link).
  2. Load your wallet — your npub's taproot address and coins appear.
  3. Enter a recipient (npub or Bitcoin address), amount, and fee rate.
  4. Build the unsigned PSBT and sign it on your device.
  5. Paste the signed transaction back and broadcast.

FAQ

Where are my keys?

Not here. nostrtx only ever sees public keys and signed transactions. Signing happens on your hardware or in your NIP-07 extension.

Can I use it without the hardware signer?

Yes — any NIP-07 extension or NIP-46 bunker works for Nostr login, and you can sign PSBTs in wallets like Sparrow by importing the PSBT. For XBT (replay-safe) transactions the signer has to understand SIGHASH_UNIFIED: the Pocket Signer, Bitcoin Knots 29.4.1+, and Shrike (the Sparrow fork) do.

What is the chain split, and why split my coins?

Since block 961,632 there are two chains with one shared history: XBT, which runs Bitcoin Knots with BIP-110 data limits and BLAKE2b proof-of-work from block 961,640, and BTC, the SHA-256d chain. Every coin from before the split exists on both, and an ordinary signature is valid on both, so a spend on one chain can be copied to the other. XBT added an opt-in signature type, SIGHASH_UNIFIED, that only verifies on XBT. nostrtx's Split button sends your unsplit coins back to yourself on XBT with that signature; once it confirms, the BTC copies are yours to spend on BTC without touching your XBT.
