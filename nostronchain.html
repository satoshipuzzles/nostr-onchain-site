NostronChain

The original Nostr Onchain client — a mobile-first wallet view that derives deterministic Bitcoin addresses from Nostr public keys. Log in with NIP-07 or just an npub, check balances, generate receive QR codes, and browse the leaderboard. Runs completely client-side.

npub loginWatch any Nostr identity's Bitcoin addresses — no keys required for viewing.
