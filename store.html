FAQ

Why a deposit and not full payment?

Hardware timelines can slip. A small deposit reserves your unit and keeps the incentive honest on both sides; the balance is due when your unit ships.

Where do my shipping details go?

Into a NIP-44 encrypted Nostr event readable only by the store key. No server, no database to breach. Delete-after-ship is the policy.

Can I pay Lightning?

On-chain only for preorders — the payment doubles as your proof of reservation, anchored where nobody can rewrite it.

What exactly ships in November?

The Pocket Signer device (ESP32-S3, AMOLED touch screen, USB-C), flashed and ready: on-device BIP-340 signing, taproot PSBT review, NIP-46 bridge, SD-card air-gap mode. Firmware is open source.
