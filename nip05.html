FAQ

Why 21,000 sats?

It's a meme, and it's enough friction to deter squatters without being meaningful money for anyone real. One-time — no annual renewal, no invoice to lose.

What if I lose my Nostr key?

You lose the identifier. There is no recovery, no operator override, no reset. Guard your key.

Can I transfer my name to someone else?

No. The name is bound to your public key at purchase time and cannot be moved. If you want to hand off, sell your key.

Can I get a refund?

Not once the name is provisioned. If the payment confirms but the claim step fails (bug, disconnect), your txid can still be used later — the server won't accept a different name on the same txid, so save it.

Where's the registry code?

Under api/nip05/ in this repo. Public snapshot at /data/nip05-snapshot.json.
